Argentina guide / Córdoba

Córdoba

University, cuarteto, fernet and Coke, and sierras a short hop away. The city is alive; the weekend leaves for Carlos Paz or the valley.

Visited by Aldo Sierras · university city and fernet

From BA Bus ~9–11 h · often overnight Flight ~1.5 h

Best time

Southern Hemisphere December is summer, July is winter.

The city works almost year-round; the sierras are better Sep–Nov and Mar–May. Avoid January in Carlos Paz (heat + crowds). Winter is dry in town, cold at night in the hills.

Getting there

Fly to COR (a real domestic hub, ~1 h 20) or bus (~9–11 h) from Retiro. A car makes sense if you are spending several days in the sierras. From COR you can connect Mendoza and the northwest without going back to Ezeiza.

What to do

  • Historic centre and the Jesuit Block (UNESCO).
  • Sierras Chicas or Calamuchita.
  • Camino de las Altas Cumbres if you drive calmly.
  • Quebrada del Condorito National Park if the plan is actual hills.

Tours and tickets (public prices)

USD at ~ARS 1,550 (BNA, Nov 2026). Pesos move: use the number to know whether you are talking about a coffee or a salary.

Quebrada del Condorito National Park (APN)

Actual sierras, condors if the day plays along. Official gate fee.

ARS 25,000 / ~USD 16 · general fee (APN from 1 Jun 2026)

Eat

Fernet and Coke (yes, it is cultural), locro, Córdoba empanadas, asado. In the sierras, goat and inn food.

Local hack

Skip a car-free Córdoba if what you wanted was the sierras: rent or take a combi. Classic tie: overnight cama bus vs a short flight.
