Argentina guide / Santa Cruz

Santa Cruz

Base town for Perito Moreno. The glacier is the reason; the town is wind, souvenirs, and lamb. I was in El Calafate. The kayak in front of the wall I booked with Patagonia Chic — a local aggregator/operator, public card. The long trekking is El Chaltén: I did not mark it.

Visited by Aldo Ice Patagonia · El Calafate

From BA Flight ~3 h Bus: no (2+ days)

Best time

Southern Hemisphere December is summer, July is winter.

Perito Moreno “works” most of the year: the boardwalks do not need crampons. Ideal Nov–Mar for daylight (and for kayak or ice). May–Aug: cold, ~7–8 h of light, some outings get cut by wind. January is expensive and packed. February is still high: summer packages often add an airport transfer. Mar–Apr eases a bit and you still have daylight. Town and operators in Calafate.

Getting there

Fly to FTE (~3 h from Buenos Aires). There is no reasonable direct bus from BA: if you don’t fly, it is a multi-day odyssey. The airport (Comandante Armando Tola) sits ~20 km east, ~20 min. Taxi/remis or a hotel transfer. If you book an outing with Patagonia Chic, the Nov 2026 public card gives the arrival FTE→hotel leg — not the return. The glacier is the other way: ~80 km west.

What to do

  • Glacier boardwalks (non-negotiable) plus the APN ticket. Caltur / Taqsa / Always Glaciers buses from the terminal: ~ARS 56,000–60,000 one way (Plataforma10, 3 Nov 2026); park extra.
  • One second plan, not three the same day: kayak (Patagonia Chic), mini trekking/safari (Hielo y Aventura), or boardwalks only.
  • El Chaltén is ~2 h 40 / 215 km if you continue — a tip, not a pin.

El Calafate: town, glacier, how to pick the day

Calafate is a base town, not a sidewalk destination. Libertador holds the grills, souvenirs, and agencies. The reason sits ~80 km away: Perito Moreno. One day covers the boardwalks; a second day covers either kayak or ice, not both.

How to reach the glacier

  • Bus — Caltur, Taqsa, Always Glaciers from the terminal. Plataforma10 on 3 Nov 2026: ~ARS 56,000–60,000 / ~USD 36–39 one way, ~1 h 15–1 h 30. Out and back the same day; confirm the last return before you disappear on the boardwalks. APN park extra (ARS 50,000 / ~USD 32 general, from 1 Jun 2026).
  • Tour with transfer — Hielo y Aventura charges mini trekking without transfer at ARS 420,000 and with transfer at ARS 500,000 (from 1 Nov 2026). Patagonia Chic adds hotel + boardwalks at ARS 90,000 on top of the kayak.
  • Car — Makes sense if you chain a second stop; for a single glacier day the bus is enough.

Kayak vs ice vs boardwalks only

  • Boardwalks — The plan that does not fail. You walk, you look at the wall, you go back. A jacket even in January.
  • Patagonia Chic — kayak — The one I used. Calafate aggregator/operator, card on patagoniachic.com. Doubles, 14+, ~1 h 30 on the Canal de los Témpanos. Summer packages (February included) often offer an airport transfer; the Nov 2026 card leaves the arrival free. Free cancel ~24 h on their public policy — verify live. No voucher, ID, or email here.
  • Hielo y Aventura — Park concessionaire. Crampons (Mini trekking / Big Ice) or the short boat (Nautical safari). A different muscle, a different fare. Cards under tours.

A short circuit that closes

  • Day 1 — FTE, town, early to bed. If the flight lands in daylight, do not stack kayak that same afternoon: wind and jet lag do not mix.
  • Day 2 — Glacier: boardwalks by bus, or kayak, or mini trekking. One.
  • Day 3 — The other product, or a flight, or the bus to Chaltén (that is already another trip: tip).

Order-of-magnitude prices, as-of Nov 2026, ~ARS 1,550/USD. Pesos move: confirm the card the day you pay.

For when you go — El Chaltén

Not a visited pin Side-trip or curiosity for readers. I did not mark this as a place I went.

The glacier is Calafate; the long trekking is Chaltén. ~2 h 40 / 215 km, buses every day (Cal Tur, Chaltén Travel, TAQSA), ~ARS 70,000 / ~USD 45 one way (Oct 2026) plus terminal tax. I did not mark it as visited. Book 2–3 nights after Perito Moreno. No airport. Better Oct–Apr for trails; May–August is cold and short on light. Not a day trip: the bus eats the daylight.

Tours and tickets (public prices)

USD at ~ARS 1,550 (BNA, Nov 2026). Pesos move: use the number to know whether you are talking about a coffee or a salary. Public operator or APN fares; if there is no sheet, GetYourGuide / Viator / local forums — not an invented price.

Los Glaciares National Park (APN)

Ticket for the Perito Moreno area. The boardwalks you do on your own. Bring a jacket even in January.

Duration your boardwalk day

Includes area admission; boat and ice extra

ARS 50,000 / ~USD 32 · general fee; Argentine residents ARS 25,000 (APN from 1 Jun 2026)

Hielo y Aventura — Mini trekking

Park concessionaire. Crampons on the ice; ages 8–65. Big Ice (harder, 15 Sep–30 Apr) ARS 550,000 + optional transfer ARS 80,000. Park and food extra.

Duration ~4 h 30 from the port; ~10 h with transfer from Calafate + boardwalks

Includes boat, ES/EN guides, technical kit; park, food, and transfer optional

ARS 420,000 / ~USD 271 · adult without transfer (ARS 500,000 with transfer) (hieloyaventura.com from 1 Nov 2026)

Hielo y Aventura — Nautical safari

The short boat along the wall, year-round. If crampons are not the day, this is the plan. Park extra. Safari Azul is a different product (ARS 140,000, limited season).

Duration short navigation (the park day is separate)

Includes boarding in front of the glacier; park extra

ARS 80,000 / ~USD 52 · adult; 20% off for Argentine seniors / Santa Cruz residents / ages 4–15 (hieloyaventura.com from 1 Nov 2026)

Patagonia Chic — Perito Moreno kayak

I booked this — public card, no codes

Calafate aggregator/operator. Double kayak on the Canal de los Témpanos, the wall in front of you. Age 14+; no experience required. I booked it as a high-season kayak (a summer / February package, with an airport-transfer option). If the plan is crampons on ice, compare with Hielo y Aventura: those are different days. The public policy and the kayak card say free cancel until 24 h before; confirm it at checkout, not in an email. I am not publishing vouchers or IDs.

Duration ~1 h 30 of kayaking (9:45–13:30 from the park); with transfer and boardwalks the day runs to ~17:30

Includes kayak, dry suit, guide, snack; the Nov 2026 card says the APN ticket is included — confirm at payment. Hotel transfer + boardwalks ARS 90,000 extra. FTE→hotel arrival: the card offers it free when you book an outing (not the return)

ARS 370,000–510,000 / ~USD 239–329 · adult 14+ starting at the park; 370,000 was an on-screen promo (27% off) vs 510,000 list — not a forever fare (patagoniachic.com card + cancellation policy, Nov 2026)

Eat

Patagonian lamb, calafate in jam or ice cream, trout. Mountain-refuge portions, not a tasting menu. Libertador is the street: grill and glacier-menu. I am not locking a restaurant that may close tomorrow.

Local hack

If the plan is ice + trail: two nights in Calafate (boardwalks or kayak) and then the bus to Chaltén (Cal Tur / Chaltén Travel / TAQSA, ~ARS 70,000 / ~USD 45 one way, Oct 2026 plus tax). Chaltén has no airport. Not a trekking day trip.
