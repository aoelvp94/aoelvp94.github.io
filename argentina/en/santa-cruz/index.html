Argentina guide / Santa Cruz

Santa Cruz

Base town for Perito Moreno. The glacier is the reason; the town is wind, souvenirs, and lamb. The long trekking is El Chaltén: I did not mark it as visited here.

Visited by Aldo Ice Patagonia · El Calafate

From BA Flight ~3 h Bus: no (2+ days)

Best time

Southern Hemisphere December is summer, July is winter.

Perito Moreno “works” most of the year. Ideal Nov–Mar for daylight (and Chaltén nearby). Avoid May–Aug if you want long treks: cold, short days, some outings get cut. January is expensive and packed.

Getting there

Fly to FTE (~3 h). There is no reasonable direct bus from Buenos Aires: if you don’t fly, it is a multi-day odyssey.

What to do

  • Glacier boardwalks (non-negotiable) plus the park ticket.
  • A boat along the wall, or ice trekking if you are fit.
  • El Chaltén is ~2 h 40 / 215 km if you continue (I did not mark it).

Tours and tickets (public prices)

USD at ~ARS 1,550 (BNA, Nov 2026). Pesos move: use the number to know whether you are talking about a coffee or a salary.

Los Glaciares National Park (APN)

Ticket for the Perito Moreno area. The boardwalks you do on your own. Bring a jacket even in January.

ARS 50,000 / ~USD 32 · general fee; Argentine residents ARS 25,000 (APN from 1 Jun 2026)

Hielo y Aventura — Mini trekking

Park concessionaire. Mini trekking year-round; Big Ice 15 Sep–30 Apr (ARS 550,000 + optional transfer). Nautical safari ARS 80,000 year-round. Park extra.

ARS 420,000 / ~USD 271 · adult, without transfer (ARS 500,000 with transfer) (hieloyaventura.com from 1 Nov 2026)

Eat

Patagonian lamb, calafate in jam or ice cream, trout. Mountain-refuge portions, not a tasting menu.

Local hack

Cal Tur / Chaltén Travel / TAQSA buses to El Chaltén, ~ARS 70,000 / ~USD 45 (Oct 2026) plus terminal tax. Book 2–3 nights in Chaltén after Perito Moreno. Not a trekking day trip.
