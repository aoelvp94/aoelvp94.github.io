Argentina guide / Santa Cruz

Santa Cruz

Base town for Perito Moreno. The glacier is the reason; the town is wind, souvenirs, and lamb. The long trekking is El Chaltén: I did not mark it as visited here.

Visited by Aldo Ice Patagonia · El Calafate

From BA Flight ~3 h Bus: no (2+ days)

Best time

Southern Hemisphere December is summer, July is winter.

Perito Moreno “works” most of the year. Ideal Nov–Mar for daylight (and Chaltén nearby). Avoid May–Aug if you want long treks: cold, short days, some outings get cut. January is expensive and packed.

Getting there

Fly to FTE (~3 h). There is no reasonable direct bus from Buenos Aires: if you don’t fly, it is a multi-day odyssey.

What to do

  • Glacier boardwalks (non-negotiable) plus the park ticket.
  • A boat along the wall, or ice trekking if you are fit.
  • El Chaltén is ~2 h 40 / 215 km if you continue (I did not mark it).

Tours and tickets (public prices)

USD at ~ARS 1,550 (BNA, Nov 2026). Pesos move: use the number to know whether you are talking about a coffee or a salary. Public operator or APN fares; if there is no sheet, GetYourGuide / Viator / local forums — not an invented price.

Los Glaciares National Park (APN)

Ticket for the Perito Moreno area. The boardwalks you do on your own. Bring a jacket even in January.

Duration your boardwalk day

Includes area admission; boat and ice extra

ARS 50,000 / ~USD 32 · general fee; Argentine residents ARS 25,000 (APN from 1 Jun 2026)

Hielo y Aventura — Mini trekking

Park concessionaire. Crampons on the ice; ages 8–65. Big Ice (harder, 15 Sep–30 Apr) ARS 550,000 + optional transfer ARS 80,000. Park and food extra.

Duration ~4 h 30 from the port; ~10 h with transfer from Calafate + boardwalks

Includes boat, ES/EN guides, technical kit; park, food, and transfer optional

ARS 420,000 / ~USD 271 · adult without transfer (ARS 500,000 with transfer) (hieloyaventura.com from 1 Nov 2026)

Hielo y Aventura — Nautical safari

The short boat along the wall, year-round. If crampons are not the day, this is the plan. Park extra. Safari Azul is a different product (ARS 140,000, limited season).

Duration short navigation (the park day is separate)

Includes boarding in front of the glacier; park extra

ARS 80,000 / ~USD 52 · adult; 20% off for Argentine seniors / Santa Cruz residents / ages 4–15 (hieloyaventura.com from 1 Nov 2026)

Patagonia Chic — Perito Moreno kayak

Double kayak on the Canal de los Témpanos, the wall in front of you. Age 14+; no experience required. Calafate aggregator/operator: if the plan is crampons on ice, compare with Hielo y Aventura. The public card offers free cancellation ~24 h — confirm it at checkout.

Duration ~1 h 30 of kayaking (9:45–13:30 from the park); with transfer and boardwalks the day runs into the afternoon

Includes kayak, dry suit, guide, snack; the card says the APN ticket is included — confirm at payment. Hotel transfer + boardwalks ARS 90,000 extra

ARS 370,000–510,000 / ~USD 239–329 · adult 14+ starting at the park; 370,000 was an on-screen promo, not a forever fare (patagoniachic.com, Nov 2026 (on-screen 370,000 vs 510,000 list))

Eat

Patagonian lamb, calafate in jam or ice cream, trout. Mountain-refuge portions, not a tasting menu.

Local hack

Cal Tur / Chaltén Travel / TAQSA buses to El Chaltén, ~ARS 70,000 / ~USD 45 (Oct 2026) plus terminal tax. Book 2–3 nights in Chaltén after Perito Moreno. Not a trekking day trip.
