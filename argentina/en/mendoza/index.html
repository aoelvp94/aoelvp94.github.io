Argentina guide / Mendoza

Mendoza

Wine and mountains. The city is flat and tree-lined; the Andes are the backdrop. If you like malbec, it is a required stop.

Visited by Aldo Cuyo · wine and the Andes

From BA Bus ~14–16 h · overnight Flight ~2 h

Best time

Southern Hemisphere December is summer, July is winter.

Ideal Oct–Nov and Mar–Apr (wineries; harvest festival around March). Avoid Dec–Feb if you don’t like 35–40 °C. Winter = skiing at Las Leñas/Penitentes: different logistics, not the wine plan.

Getting there

Fly to MDZ (~1 h 50) or bus (~14–16 h). Car or bike in Maipú; for Valle de Uco you want a tour or a driver: the wine does not drive.

What to do

  • Wineries in Luján de Cuyo, Maipú, or Valle de Uco.
  • Cerro de la Gloria, in Parque San Martín: the Army of the Andes monument. Drive or walk up; this city’s San Martín, not only wine. Card on the hub.
  • Uspallata and the high Andes; Aconcagua from the park (look, you do not have to climb).
  • The Santiago crossing via Cristo Redentor when the pass is open.

Must-try

A local cheat sheet, not a paid list. Aldo was here is the visited pin for this page. Tip only — not a map pin is a reader tip: existing tip blocks below stay tips. Public fares only when a sheet is known; otherwise no invented number.

  • Wineries in Luján de Cuyo Aldo was here

    Why it matters The first valley, close to the city. Actual malbec, not only a label. One or two wineries with lunch beat five standing tastings. Trout & Wine / Ampora publish a USD sheet; confirm the day.

  • Maipú by bike Aldo was here

    Why it matters The cheap classic: a bike among small wineries and olive groves, a remise back. I am not inventing a rental number I have not seen on a sheet. The wine does not drive.

  • Uco Valley Aldo was here

    Why it matters Higher, further, more Andes in the frame. A tour or a driver; it is not Maipú. High-altitude whites and estate malbecs. A full day.

  • Parque San Martín and Cerro de la Gloria Aldo was here

    Why it matters The city’s lung and the Army of the Andes monument. Drive or walk up. This city’s San Martín, not only wine. Irrigation channels and plane trees below.

  • Plaza Independencia and the acequias Aldo was here

    Why it matters A flat, tree-lined city, snowmelt in the gutters. Walkable. The Andes are the backdrop when the haze lifts.

  • Uspallata, Puente del Inca, and Aconcagua (look) Aldo was here

    Why it matters High Andes on RN7 toward Chile. Aconcagua park you visit without climbing the summit. Puente del Inca is a stained thermal ruin; the pass decides. A jacket even in January.

  • Goat, Mendoza empanadas, olives, malbec Aldo was here

    Why it matters The Cuyo table. Baked empanadas, goat, oil. Toast with malbec; torrontés if you came from the north; a Uco white if the heat hits.

  • Harvest festival (around March) Aldo was here

    Why it matters The provincial festival: events in the Greek theatre, grapes, crowds. If you hit it, it is a plus; if not, the wineries stay open. Confirm that year’s official calendar.

  • Cacheuta (hot springs, short day) Tip only — not a map pin

    Why it matters Hot springs toward the foothills, ~40 km. A water tip, not a wine pin. A paid complex; confirm on the ground. It does not replace Uco.

  • The Santiago crossing (Cristo Redentor) Tip only — not a map pin

    Why it matters When the pass is open: Andesmar / CATA / El Rápido bus ~7–8 h. In 2026 it was closed ~90 days. Not a same-day shopping tour. Detail in the hack.

  • Potrerillos Aldo was here

    Why it matters A foothill reservoir, ~60 km. Kayak, sunset, wind. A short day if Uco does not fit; not Aconcagua high Andes. Car or a tour.

  • Villavicencio (reserve and historic hotel) Tip only — not a map pin

    Why it matters North of the city, a nature reserve and the water-label hotel. A half-day tip; confirm access. It does not replace Luján de Cuyo.

A known operator, small groups, English guide. Luján from USD 220; Uco from USD 230 (operator sheet). Pickup ~8:30–9:00, back in the afternoon. To pay in pesos, contact them first. Private deluxe is a different fare (USD 360/370, double occupancy). In Maipú, bike + a remise back is still the cheap classic — I am not inventing a rental number I have not seen on a sheet.

Eat

Asado, baked Mendoza empanadas, goat, olives and oil. Cuyo table, not a generic steakhouse. One winery with lunch beats five standing tastings.

Toast with malbec; torrontés if you came from the north; a high-altitude white in Uco if the heat hits. The wine does not drive: bike in Maipú, a tour or a driver in Uco.

Local hack

Andesmar / CATA / El Rápido buses: Mendoza → Santiago Terminal Sur, ~7–8 h, from ~ARS 45,000–70,000 (~USD 30–45, press Nov 2026). MDZ–SCL flight (~1 h, JetSMART/SKY/LATAM) when the pass shuts. In 2026 Cristo Redentor was closed ~90 days. Not a same-day shopping tour.
