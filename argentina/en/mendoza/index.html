Argentina guide / Mendoza

Mendoza

Wine and mountains. The city is flat and tree-lined; the Andes are the backdrop. If you like malbec, it is a required stop.

Visited by Aldo Cuyo · wine and the Andes

From BA Bus ~14–16 h · overnight Flight ~2 h

Best time

Southern Hemisphere December is summer, July is winter.

Ideal Oct–Nov and Mar–Apr (wineries; harvest festival around March). Avoid Dec–Feb if you don’t like 35–40 °C. Winter = skiing at Las Leñas/Penitentes: different logistics, not the wine plan.

Getting there

Fly to MDZ (~1 h 50) or bus (~14–16 h). Car or bike in Maipú; for Valle de Uco you want a tour or a driver: the wine does not drive.

What to do

  • Wineries in Luján de Cuyo, Maipú, or Valle de Uco.
  • Uspallata and the high Andes; Aconcagua from the park (look, you do not have to climb).
  • The Santiago crossing via Cristo Redentor when the pass is open.

Tours and tickets (public prices)

USD at ~ARS 1,550 (BNA, Nov 2026). Pesos move: use the number to know whether you are talking about a coffee or a salary.

Trout & Wine (small wine tours)

A known operator, English/Spanish. You can also go straight to wineries with a booking. In Maipú, bike + a remise back is the cheap classic. I am not inventing a day rate that is not on their sheet.

moves with wineries and group size (confirm at troutandwine.com)

Eat

Asado, Mendoza empanadas, goat, olives. Toast with malbec, torrontés if you came from the north, or a high-altitude white in Uco.

Local hack

Andesmar / CATA / El Rápido buses: Mendoza → Santiago Terminal Sur, ~7–8 h, from ~ARS 45,000–70,000 (~USD 30–45, press Nov 2026). MDZ–SCL flight (~1 h, JetSMART/SKY/LATAM) when the pass shuts. In 2026 Cristo Redentor was closed ~90 days. Not a same-day shopping tour.
