Argentina guide / Curiosity

Curiosity / for when you go

14 reader tips: side trips and underrated spots tied to pages I already cover, or provinces I have not marked. The map does not change. No invented itineraries.

These are not visited pins Except Catamarca (I have been; the west is another hop). Corrientes, Formosa, Neuquén, San Juan, and La Rioja stay unpainted. USD at ~ARS 1,550 (BNA, Nov 2026). Public / approximate — confirm the week you go.

Northwest

Tafí del Valle (Tucumán)

Tip · ~2,000 m · not visited by Aldo

~107–126 km / ~2 h 30 by bus from San Miguel via RN38 and RP307 (Quebrada de los Sosa). Transporte Aconquija, ARS 18,000–20,000 / ~USD 12–13 one-way (Jul–Nov 2026, public card). Cool when the valley boils. From Oct 2026 the provincial bus is card/QR. Detail on the Tucumán page.

Cafayate / Cachi (Salta)

Nuance · two valleys, not an extra pin

Salta city, yes. Cafayate (~190 km on RN68, Quebrada de las Conchas, torrontés) and Cachi (~160 km on RN33 / Cuesta del Obispo, Cardones) are different days: Movitrack’s classic sheet prices them the same (ARS 77,500 / ~USD 50, Nov 2026); the landscape does not. Do not squeeze both into one day “because they are on the map.” Tours and Cardones on the Salta page — I am not repeating the operator here.

Iruya (Salta)

Tip · ~2,780 m · not visited by Aldo

The Salta mountain village in the middle of the hills, north of the Quebrada — not Cafayate. You reach it from Humahuaca (Jujuy): ~73–75 km / ~3 h, gravel and Abra del Cóndor ~4,000 m. From Salta city ~318–320 km with a transfer; no direct bus. Transporte Iruya publishes times; confirm the fare at the window — I am not inventing a ticket. Better Apr–Nov. Salta · Iruya.

Purmamarca vs Salinas Grandes

Nuance · Quebrada ≠ puna

Purmamarca is the town and the hill (~65 km from San Salvador). Salinas Grandes is another day: Cuesta de Lipán, ~3,450 m, jacket and water. Not “the same postcard.” The Chile bus via Jama leaves Purmamarca before dawn: that is a hack, not a salt-flat day trip. Cards on Jujuy and the hub hacks.

Western Catamarca (Belén / Fiambalá)

Valley visited · the west is another hop

I have been to Catamarca. San Fernando del Valle is the hot base; Belén, Tinogasta, Fiambalá and Campo de Piedra Pómez are high desert, long distances, local operations. Not a day trip from the valley and not an extra pin. Confirm permits and weather on the ground; I am not locking a lodge. Catamarca page.

Centre

Córdoba sierras

Tip · Punilla, Calamuchita, Traslasierra

I marked the capital; not every hill town. Carlos Paz ~36–40 km / ARS 6,500 / ~USD 4 (ERSEP 1 Oct 2026). One day covers Carlos Paz or Alta Gracia (~ARS 5,700 / ~USD 4, confirm at the window); Traslasierra (~3 h, ~ARS 36,000 / ~USD 23 to Mina Clavero) is not a day trip. Distances and fares on Córdoba.

Litoral / humid north

Iberá (Corrientes)

Not yet · wetlands

Colonia Carlos Pellegrini is the classic base, not Corrientes city. 4×4, capybaras, caimans, mosquitoes. Better in the dry (Apr–Oct). Corrientes carnival is a different, urban film. I have not been to the province: a tip, not a pin. I am not inventing a safari or a lodge.

Formosa / Bañado La Estrella

Not yet · northern border

Wetland, heat, its own logistics, Paraguay next door. Exotic for the classic circuit; I have not been. Confirm local operators / GetYourGuide if you insist — I am not inventing a safari or a phone number.

Cuyo (not yet)

Ischigualasto / Talampaya

Not yet · northern Cuyo

Valle de la Luna (San Juan) and Talampaya (La Rioja) are usually done together; I have not been to either province. Same dry logic as Mendoza: autumn–spring beats January heat. One park is provincial and the other national: confirm the fee and time slot on the ground — I am not inventing a ticket.

Patagonia

Trevelin / Esquel

Tip · Andean Chubut · not visited

The other Welsh town: Andes, not Madryn. ~25 km from Esquel; ~4–5 h / ~280–320 km from Bariloche on RN40. Gaiman (tea near Trelew) is the Atlantic side. Confirm the Bariloche–Esquel bus on Plataforma10 / Vía Bariloche — I am not publishing a cart I have not seen. Chubut and Bariloche.

Península Valdés vs Madryn

Nuance · the base is not the peninsula

I marked Puerto Madryn. Península Valdés / Puerto Pirámides is ~95 km away: that is where the boat is (Jun–Dec; peak Sep–Oct). El Doradillo is whales from the shore, cheaper, when the season lines up. Protected-area ticket ~ARS 50,000 / ~USD 32 (foreign visitors, 2026 season) plus the boat. Do not mix it up with Trevelin. Cards on Chubut — I am not copying the Hydro Sport block.

El Chaltén / Fitz Roy

Tip · Santa Cruz · not visited

The glacier is Calafate; the long trekking is Chaltén. ~2 h 40 / 215 km, buses every day (Cal Tur, Chaltén Travel, TAQSA), ~ARS 70,000 / ~USD 45 one way (Oct 2026) plus terminal tax. I did not mark it. Not a day trip: the bus eats the daylight. Better Oct–Apr for trails. Detail on Santa Cruz.

Villa La Angostura / San Martín de los Andes

Not yet · Neuquén

I marked Bariloche in Río Negro. Villa La Angostura (~80 km) and San Martín de los Andes (Seven Lakes road) are already Neuquén: they stay unpainted. A car helps; in winter, chains. Confirm buses on Plataforma10 — I am not inventing that week’s fare. Bariloche.

Los Alerces National Park

Tip · Andean Chubut · not visited

Next to Esquel, not Madryn. Lakes and ancient alerce trees; Trevelin (mountain Welsh tea) is a short hop. I did not mark it. APN gate: confirm this year’s fee on the parks site — I am not copying a number I do not have in this guide. Chain it with Trevelin/Esquel, not with whales. Chubut.
