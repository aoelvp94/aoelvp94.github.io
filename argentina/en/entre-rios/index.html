Argentina guide / Entre Ríos

Entre Ríos

Rivers, islands, carnival, and hot springs. Long-weekend Argentina more than an international postcard.

Visited by Aldo Litoral · rivers, hot springs, carnival

From BA Bus ~3–6 h Depends on the town

Best time

Southern Hemisphere December is summer, July is winter.

Hot springs year-round. Ideal Sep–Nov and Mar–May for the river without the steam. Gualeguaychú carnival: Jan–Feb, that’s the point. Avoid January if you want quiet (and hate mosquitoes).

Getting there

Bus from Retiro (~3–6 h depending on the town) or drive RN14. Occasional flights to Paraná (PRA).

What to do

  • Hot springs (Federación, Colón, Villa Elisa): one complex, not three. Federación is a new town on the lake; Colón is a waterfront.
  • Colón: waterfront and river beaches; San José if Colón is packed; a logical base for El Palmar.
  • Concepción del Uruguay: old town and port; the base for Palacio San José.
  • Gualeguaychú carnival in season (Jan–Feb); off season it is a river town and a bridge to Uruguay.
  • Palacio San José near Concepción del Uruguay.
  • El Palmar National Park if you are near Colón (yatay palms, viscachas at dusk).

Must-try

A local cheat sheet, not a paid list. Aldo was here is the visited pin for this page. Tip only — not a map pin is a reader tip: existing tip blocks below stay tips. Public fares only when a sheet is known; otherwise no invented number.

  • Hot springs (Federación, Colón, Villa Elisa) Aldo was here

    Why it matters The long-weekend product. Water, a thermal park, a night in a river town. Pick one complex, not three. Year-round; summer is crowds and mosquitoes.

  • Colón: waterfront and river beaches Aldo was here

    Why it matters The most walkable town on the corridor. River sand, not ocean. A logical base for El Palmar. Ice cream and a waterfront sunset beat a city tour.

  • El Palmar National Park Aldo was here

    Why it matters Yatay palms near Colón. Trails, viscachas at dusk, the palm that marks this province. Car or a short tour; it is not Iguazú jungle.

    ARS 25,000 / ~USD 16 · general fee; Argentine residents ARS 12,000 (APN from 1 Jun 2026)

  • Palacio San José Aldo was here

    Why it matters Urquiza’s estancia near Concepción del Uruguay. Actual Argentine history, not a museum invented for the coach. Confirm hours on the provincial tourism site.

  • Gualeguaychú carnival Aldo was here

    Why it matters Jan–Feb, the corsódromo, the province’s other face. That is the point: crowds, cumbia, heat. Off season Gualeguaychú is a river town and a bridge to Uruguay.

  • River fish, citrus, salami and cheese Aldo was here

    Why it matters Litoral table, not guidebook asado. Oranges and mandarins from the Uruguay shore; farm cured meats. Ask for sábalo or surubí when it is on.

  • The bridge to Uruguay Tip only — not a map pin

    Why it matters If you are already in the province, Fray Bentos / Paysandú are a bridge, not a flight. The EGA BA–Montevideo bus uses this corridor. A crossing tip; passport.

  • Concordia and the Salto Grande lake Tip only — not a map pin

    Why it matters Further north on the corridor: citrus, a dammed river, its own hot springs. A tip if the bus drops you there; not the first town if you are coming for a CABA weekend.

  • Concepción del Uruguay (old town and port) Aldo was here

    Why it matters A historic town on the Uruguay river, the base for Palacio San José. Old town, waterfront, another scale from Colón. A town day, not a hot-spring day.

  • Federación hot springs Aldo was here

    Why it matters The most built-up complex on the northern corridor: a new town (the old one sits under the lake), water, a thermal night. Pick Federación or Colón, not both the same weekend.

  • San José beaches (Entre Ríos) Tip only — not a map pin

    Why it matters River sand between Colón and El Palmar. A smaller beach town than Colón. A shore tip if Colón is packed in January.

Tours and tickets (public prices)

USD at ~ARS 1,550 (BNA, Nov 2026). Pesos move: use the number to know whether you are talking about a coffee or a salary. Public operator or APN fares; if there is no sheet, GetYourGuide / Viator / local forums — not an invented price.

El Palmar National Park (APN)

Yatay palms near Colón. Official gate fee.

ARS 25,000 / ~USD 16 · general fee (APN from 1 Jun 2026)

Eat

River fish (sábalo, surubí when available), citrus from the Uruguay shore, farm salami and cheese. Less guidebook asado, more family table.

In Colón, waterfront ice cream closes the day. Carnival is another diet (and another timetable): if that is why you came, do not stack a thermal morning after as if nothing happened.

Local hack

The EGA Buenos Aires→Montevideo bus uses this corridor (Gualeguaychú / Fray Bentos). If you are already in the province, Uruguay is a bridge, not a flight.
