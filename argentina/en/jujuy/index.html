Argentina guide / Jujuy

Jujuy

The north flips the script: colored hills, Andean towns, and a slower tempo than Buenos Aires. The Quebrada de Humahuaca is UNESCO-listed and it shows. Give yourself time: the altitude is real.

Visited by Aldo Northwest · Quebrada de Humahuaca

From BA Bus ~20–22 h · overnight Flight ~2 h

Best time

Southern Hemisphere December is summer, July is winter.

Ideal May–Oct in the Quebrada: clear skies, freezing nights, altitude. Avoid Dec–Mar if you want dry roads (rain, occasional closures). A jacket after 6 p.m. is not optional.

Getting there

Fly to JUJ (Horacio Guzmán) from AEP/EZE (~2 h) or a long-distance bus from Retiro (~20–22 h). Plenty of people combine Salta + Jujuy by car or tour. From Purmamarca the bus to San Pedro de Atacama runs Paso de Jama.

What to do

  • Purmamarca and Cerro de los Siete Colores, Tilcara, Humahuaca.
  • Salinas Grandes if the weather plays along (full day with the Quebrada).
  • Calilegua if you want yungas jungle instead of puna (national park, APN general fee).

Tours and tickets (public prices)

USD at ~ARS 1,550 (BNA, Nov 2026). Pesos move: use the number to know whether you are talking about a coffee or a salary. Public operator or APN fares; if there is no sheet, GetYourGuide / Viator / local forums — not an invented price.

Movitrack — Quebrada de Humahuaca

Classic departure from Salta (they also have an office in San Salvador de Jujuy): Purmamarca, Tilcara, Humahuaca. A long day; lunch and museum/pucará tickets are usually extra. If you are already in the Quebrada, a car beats bouncing back to Salta.

Movitrack — Salinas Grandes

Salt flats via Purmamarca / Cuesta de Lipán. Altitude: jacket and water. Safari a las Nubes (4×4 + Polvorilla + salt flats, a very long day) is not on that same sheet: confirm it with Movitrack or on GetYourGuide/Viator — not an invented fare.

Duration full day (~13 h from Salta)

Includes transfer and guide; meals and Ojos del Salar extra

ARS 97,500 / ~USD 63 · per person, operator classic sheet (movitrack.com.ar/excursiones-clasicas, Nov 2026)

Calilegua National Park (APN)

Yungas jungle, not puna. Official gate fee; you do not need a tour for the open trails.

Duration your park day

Includes open trails; no boat or 4×4 included

ARS 25,000 / ~USD 16 · general fee (foreign visitors); Argentine residents ~ARS 12,000 (APN from 1 Jun 2026)

Eat

Humita, tamales, Jujuy-style empanadas, locro, llama on some menus. Api with pastelitos if you hit a market day or winter.

Local hack

The Chile crossing from here is Paso de Jama, not Cristo Redentor. Andesmar leaves Salta ~1:00, Jujuy ~2:45, Purmamarca ~3:40, San Pedro ~11:30 (Oct 2026). ~USD 75–100. Not a day trip. Detail on the hub hacks.
