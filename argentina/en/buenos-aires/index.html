Argentina guide / Buenos Aires Province

Buenos Aires Province

The province is not only the coast: towns, farmland, Tandil, small sierras, and a more Argentine corner-store scale. Mar del Plata is the mass beach resort Argentines actually use: in summer a beautiful mess; off season, harbour and wind.

Visited by Aldo Province · hinterland, Tigre, coast

From BA 1–6 h from CABA Train / bus / car

Best time

Southern Hemisphere December is summer, July is winter.

Tigre almost year-round; Tandil and the small sierras better Oct–Nov and Mar–Apr. Mar del Plata: ideal Nov or Mar. Avoid January on the coast (the average Argentine goes then; RN2 fills up). Winter in MdP: harbour and wind, many hotels shuttered.

Getting there

Train, bus, or car from CABA. MdP: bus from Retiro (~5–6 h, Tony Tur / Plusmar), train from Constitución when it runs, or a flight to MDQ (~1 h). In January the bus fills: book.

What to do

  • Tigre: Puerto de Frutos and a boat into the Delta (back for dinner).
  • La Plata: architecture and the natural-science museum.
  • Tandil or Sierra de la Ventana for air; weekend estancias.
  • Mar del Plata: rambla and beaches (Playa Grande, Bristol), the port and sea lions, Barrio Los Troncos.

Tours and tickets (public prices)

There is no single operator here I can name with a public rate sheet. Park or outing: confirm on the ground and read this season’s reviews.

Eat

Tandil cheese and salami, town bakeries, countryside asado. In MdP: seafood and a geological portion of milanesa napolitana; churros on the rambla; the port for simpler fish.

Local hack

Tandil is not a day trip (~5 h by bus). A paid city tour to Tigre is surplus if the train leaves every few minutes. Greater Buenos Aires is a different film: go with a local.
