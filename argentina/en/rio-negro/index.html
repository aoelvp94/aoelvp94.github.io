Argentina guide / Río Negro

Río Negro

Lakes, forest, chocolate, and a town that fills with graduating-class trips in high season. The landscape forgives the rest. I marked Bariloche in Río Negro; Villa La Angostura would already be Neuquén.

Visited by Aldo Lake Patagonia · Bariloche

From BA Bus ~20–24 h · overnight Flight ~2 h

Best time

Southern Hemisphere December is summer, July is winter.

Lakes/trekking Dec–Mar (best Jan–Mar; Nov–Dec is school-leavers). Ski Jun–Sep. Don’t expect both in one trip. Autumn (Apr–May) is quieter and wetter.

Getting there

Fly to BRC (~2 h, many frequencies) or a ~20–24 h bus. A car for the loops; in winter, chains and patience.

What to do

  • Circuito Chico, Cerro Catedral (trekking or skiing).
  • A boat on Nahuel Huapi.
  • Seven Lakes road toward Villa La Angostura if you have a car (Neuquén: I did not mark it).

Tours and tickets (public prices)

USD at ~ARS 1,550 (BNA, Nov 2026). Pesos move: use the number to know whether you are talking about a coffee or a salary.

Nahuel Huapi National Park (APN)

The park around Bariloche. Official gate fee; Circuito Chico is car, bike, or tourist bus.

ARS 35,000 / ~USD 23 · general fee; Argentine residents ~ARS 15,000 (APN from 1 Jun 2026)

Cruce Andino (three lakes)

Nahuel Huapi, Frías, Todos los Santos. It is not a bus. Parks and lunch extra. It runs most days; lake weather decides.

USD 355 non-resident / 277 resident · published by the operator (2026–28 fare)

Eat

Chocolate (Rapa Nui at Mitre and Villegas is a shop-window classic), trout, lamb, craft beer. Chocolate tea after a hill is part of the ritual.

Local hack

Two different Chile products: the bus via Paso Cardenal Samoré to Osorno / Puerto Montt (~6–8 h, ~USD 25–45) is transport. Cruce Andino is the three-lake outing (~12 h).
