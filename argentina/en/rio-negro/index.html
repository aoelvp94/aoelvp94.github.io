Argentina guide / Río Negro

Río Negro

Lakes, forest, chocolate, and a town that fills with graduating-class trips in high season. The landscape forgives the rest. I marked Bariloche in Río Negro; Villa La Angostura would already be Neuquén.

Visited by Aldo Lake Patagonia · Bariloche

From BA Bus ~20–24 h · overnight Flight ~2 h

Best time

Southern Hemisphere December is summer, July is winter.

Lakes/trekking Dec–Mar (best Jan–Mar; Nov–Dec is school-leavers). Ski Jun–Sep. Don’t expect both in one trip. Autumn (Apr–May) is quieter and wetter.

Getting there

Fly to BRC (~2 h, many frequencies) or a ~20–24 h bus. A car for the loops; in winter, chains and patience.

What to do

  • Circuito Chico (Campanario, Llao Llao, Bahía López) — car, bike, or bus; the outing that explains Bariloche in a morning.
  • Cerro Catedral: trekking or skiing, not both in one trip. Colonia Suiza if you hit a curanto day.
  • A boat on Nahuel Huapi (Isla Victoria and Arrayanes).
  • Cerro Tronador is a long day, another valley. Seven Lakes / Villa La Angostura would already be Neuquén: I did not mark it.

Must-try

A local cheat sheet, not a paid list. Aldo was here is the visited pin for this page. Tip only — not a map pin is a reader tip: existing tip blocks below stay tips. Public fares only when a sheet is known; otherwise no invented number.

  • Circuito Chico Aldo was here

    Why it matters Campanario, Llao Llao, Bahía López. Car, bike, or tourist bus (Turisur). The outing that explains Bariloche in a morning. Nahuel Huapi park extra.

    ARS 40,500 / ~USD 26 · Turisur adult bus; cheaper by car or bike · APN extra (turisur.com.ar land fares 2026)

  • Cerro Campanario Aldo was here

    Why it matters The short lookout on Circuito Chico. Chairlift extra from the bus. If the legs allow, walk up. The lake in front is the reason for the photo.

  • Cerro Catedral (trekking or skiing) Aldo was here

    Why it matters In summer, a pedestrian day / trails; in winter, ski. Do not expect both in one trip. ChipCard extra. Weather decides what is open.

    ARS 90,000 / ~USD 58 · pedestrian-day flat fare; skier pass is a different fare (2026 press ~ARS 160,000) (catedralaltapatagonia.com/tarifas, 2026)

  • Nahuel Huapi boat (Isla Victoria and Arrayanes) Aldo was here

    Why it matters Catamaran from Puerto Pañuelo. Arrayanes ~45 min, Victoria ~2 h 30. Park and boarding tax (ARS 7,600) at the port. The arrayán forest is the reason.

    ARS 140,000 / ~USD 90 · Cau Cau / Turisur adult; children/Argentine seniors ARS 70,000 · APN extra (islavictoriayarrayanes.com / Turisur, from 1 Jun 2026)

  • Nahuel Huapi National Park Aldo was here

    Why it matters The park around town. Official gate fee; Circuito Chico, Tronador, and the lake live inside it. Not a municipal garden.

    ARS 35,000 / ~USD 23 · general fee; Argentine residents ~ARS 15,000 (APN from 1 Jun 2026)

  • Colonia Suiza Aldo was here

    Why it matters A village on Circuito Chico: curanto when it is on, beer, a fair. More town than lookout. A table tip if you hit a curanto day; the rest of the week is quiet.

  • Chocolate (Rapa Nui) and craft beer Aldo was here

    Why it matters Rapa Nui at Mitre and Villegas is the shop-window classic. Chocolate tea after a hill is ritual. Lake beer at night. Lamb and trout on a serious menu.

  • Cerro Tronador Aldo was here

    Why it matters Volcano/glacier on the Argentine side, a long day. Park extra. Turisur 2026 sheet. Not Circuito Chico: another valley and another departure time.

    ARS 81,000 / ~USD 52 · Turisur adult; infant 0–3 free · APN extra (turisur.com.ar land fares 2026)

  • Refugio Frey Tip only — not a map pin

    Why it matters The classic granite trek from Catedral. A long day, snow possible outside summer. I did not mark it as a separate pin: a trail tip, not a checklist I walked no matter what. Confirm the mountain report.

  • Seven Lakes / Villa La Angostura Tip only — not a map pin

    Why it matters The road toward Neuquén. I marked Bariloche; Angostura would already be another province. A car. A tip, not a pin. Trevelin/Esquel neither: see tips.

  • Civic Centre and the Nahuel Huapi waterfront Aldo was here

    Why it matters The town floor: square, wooden cathedral, the lake photo. Walkable. In graduating-class season it is packed; early pays off.

  • Cerro Otto Aldo was here

    Why it matters The town’s other hill, smaller than Catedral. Cable car, a revolving café, sunset. A short day if Catedral is packed or shut for wind.

For when you go — Trevelin / Esquel

If the plan is to keep west of the lakes: Esquel and Trevelin (Andean Chubut, mountain Welsh tea). ~4–5 h from Bariloche. I did not mark them as visited — nor Villa La Angostura (that would already be Neuquén). Confirm buses on Plataforma10; I am not inventing that week’s fare.

Tours and tickets (public prices)

USD at ~ARS 1,550 (BNA, Nov 2026). Pesos move: use the number to know whether you are talking about a coffee or a salary. Public operator or APN fares; if there is no sheet, GetYourGuide / Viator / local forums — not an invented price.

Nahuel Huapi National Park (APN)

The park around Bariloche. Official gate fee; Circuito Chico is car, bike, or tourist bus. The boat and Catedral are extra.

Duration your park day

Includes admission; boat and chairlift extra

ARS 35,000 / ~USD 23 · general fee; Argentine residents ~ARS 15,000 (APN from 1 Jun 2026)

Turisur — Circuito Chico

Classic tourist bus (Campanario, Llao Llao, Bahía López). Cheaper by car or bike. Tangol lists the same product higher (ARS 50,000 Spanish / 116,666 with an English guide): compare. Campanario chairlift extra.

Duration ~4.5 h (8:30–13:00 on the Turisur 2026 sheet)

Includes bus and guide; park and Campanario extra

ARS 40,500 / ~USD 26 · adult; infant 0–3 free (turisur.com.ar land fares 2026)

Cau Cau / Turisur — Isla Victoria and Arrayanes

Nahuel Huapi boat from Puerto Pañuelo. APN park fee and boarding tax (ARS 7,600) are paid at the port. Optional land transfer ARS 22,000. Resellers (Tangol) quote a fatter package: the boat sheet is this one.

Duration afternoon ~12:15–18:30, or full day with transfer 10:20–18:30

Includes catamaran, Arrayanes ~45 min, Isla Victoria ~2 h 30; park and tax extra

ARS 140,000 / ~USD 90 · adult; children/Argentine seniors ARS 70,000 (islavictoriayarrayanes.com / Turisur, from 1 Jun 2026)

Cerro Catedral — pedestrian day

Pedestrian up and down (Tour Catedral / Telecabina Amancay depending on the day’s operation). Not a guided tour: it is the lift. The skier pass is a different fare (2026 press ~ARS 160,000). ChipCard extra. Weather decides what is open.

Duration the lift day (often 9–16)

Includes one pedestrian up and down; ski and parking extra

ARS 90,000 / ~USD 58 · flat fare; infants 0–5 free (catedralaltapatagonia.com/tarifas, 2026)

Cruce Andino (three lakes)

Nahuel Huapi, Frías, Todos los Santos toward Peulla/Puerto Varas. It is not the Samoré bus. Parks and lunch extra. It runs most days; the lake decides.

Duration ~12 h

Includes crossing buses + boats; parks, lunch, and taxes extra

USD 355 non-resident / 277 resident · ~ARS 550,000 / 429,000 at this guide’s FX · published by the operator in USD (cruceandino.com 2026–28 fare)

Turisur — Cerro Tronador

Includes bus and guide; park extra

Extremo Sur / Aguas Blancas — rafting

Recommendation · Río Manso; no 2026 sheet

Two Manso locals (30+ seasons between them). Half-day Villegas (families, 5+) or the long Frontera day (14+). extremosur.com and aguasblancas.com.ar do not publish a 2026 fare: I am not copying 2024 press. Confirm on the site. Nahuel Huapi park extra if it applies.

Season better Dec–Mar (water and sun); the river runs more months

Duration half day (Villegas) or ~9–19 (Frontera)

Includes river kit and, per the card, transfer; lunch extra

no public 2026 fare — confirm on extremosur.com or aguasblancas.com.ar (extremosur.com / aguasblancas.com.ar, Nov 2026)

Eat

Chocolate (Rapa Nui at Mitre and Villegas is the shop-window classic) and chocolate tea after a hill: ritual, not extra dessert. Lamb and trout on a serious menu.

Lake craft beer at night. Curanto in Colonia Suiza when it is on. Refuge portions; in high season the town fills with graduating-class trips — book a table.

Local hack

Two different Chile products: the bus via Paso Cardenal Samoré to Osorno / Puerto Montt (~6–8 h, ~USD 25–45) is transport. Cruce Andino is the three-lake outing (~12 h).
