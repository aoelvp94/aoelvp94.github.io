Argentina guide / Chubut

Chubut

Cold sea, wind, and wildlife. The town is a base; Península Valdés is the reason. Puerto Pirámides is ~95 km away.

Visited by Aldo Atlantic Patagonia · Puerto Madryn

From BA Bus ~18–20 h · overnight Flight ~2 h + 1 h transfer

Best time

Southern Hemisphere December is summer, July is winter.

Southern right whales Jun–Dec (2026 season from 9 Jun; peak Sep–Oct). Penguins more like Sep–Mar. El Doradillo, from the coast, Jun–Oct. Don’t plan a Feb–May safari expecting whales: they aren’t there.

Getting there

Fly to REL (Trelew, ~2 h) + transfer (~1 h) or an overnight bus from Buenos Aires (~18–20 h). A car helps on the peninsula.

What to do

  • Southern right whale watching from Puerto Pirámides (the port closes in wind).
  • Penguins in season, elephant seals.
  • Gaiman if you want Welsh tea.

For when you go — Trevelin (the other Chubut)

Not a visited pin Side-trip or curiosity for readers. I did not mark this as a place I went.

I marked Puerto Madryn (Atlantic). Trevelin is the west: a Welsh town ~25 km from Esquel, Andes, not whales. Not a visited pin. From Bariloche it is ~280–320 km on RN40 (~4–5 h). Gaiman (Welsh tea near Trelew) is on the Madryn side; don’t mix it up with Trevelin. Bariloche–Esquel bus: confirm on Plataforma10 / Vía Bariloche that week — I am not publishing a cart I have not seen.

Tours and tickets (public prices)

USD at ~ARS 1,550 (BNA, Nov 2026). Pesos move: use the number to know whether you are talking about a coffee or a salary. Public operator or APN fares; if there is no sheet, GetYourGuide / Viator / local forums — not an invented price.

Hydro Sport — whale watching

Boat from Puerto Pirámides (not from Madryn). Season Jun–Dec; the port closes in wind. Other boats at the pier (Southern Spirit, Tito Bottazzi and more) and 2026 press talks about unified fares: compare, and do not chase the animals. Madryn–Pirámides transfer extra.

Duration 1 h 30 on the water

Includes boarding at Pirámides; transfer and Valdés ticket extra

ARS 150,000–195,000 / ~USD 97–126 · low 15 Jun–31 Aug ARS 150,000 · high from 1 Sep ARS 195,000 (adults) (hydrosport.ar/eng/tarifas + unified press Sep 2026)

Península Valdés protected-area ticket

2026 protected-area ticket. Does not include the boat or the bus to Pirámides. El Doradillo (whales from the shore) is a different, cheaper plan when tide and season line up.

Eat

Lamb, hake, king crab when it is around, Welsh cake in Gaiman. The seafood is fresher than fancy.

Local hack

A car (the sane option) or the Mar y Valle bus to Pirámides. Do not squeeze the whole peninsula into a morning without a car: the animals are far apart.
