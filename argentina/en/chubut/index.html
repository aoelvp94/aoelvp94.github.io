Argentina guide / Chubut

Chubut

Cold sea, wind, and wildlife. The town is a base; Península Valdés is the reason. Puerto Pirámides is ~95 km away.

Visited by Aldo Atlantic Patagonia · Puerto Madryn

From BA Bus ~18–20 h · overnight Flight ~2 h + 1 h transfer

Best time

Southern Hemisphere December is summer, July is winter.

Southern right whales Jun–Dec (2026 season from 9 Jun; peak Sep–Oct). Penguins more like Sep–Mar. El Doradillo, from the coast, Jun–Oct. Don’t plan a Feb–May safari expecting whales: they aren’t there.

Getting there

Fly to REL (Trelew, ~2 h) + transfer (~1 h) or an overnight bus from Buenos Aires (~18–20 h). A car helps on the peninsula.

What to do

  • Península Valdés: steppe and wildlife far apart — a car or a land day, not a morning without one.
  • Southern right whale watching from Puerto Pirámides (Jun–Dec; the port closes in wind). El Doradillo, from the shore, when the tide is right.
  • Punta Loma (sea lions) near Madryn; Gaiman for Welsh tea (Atlantic, not Trevelin).
  • Punta Tombo (penguins) and Trevelin are another hop — tips, not pins.

  • El Doradillo (whales from the shore) Aldo was here

    Why it matters Minutes from Madryn, southern right whales metres away when tide and season line up (Jun–Oct). Cheaper than the boat; it does not replace the boat if the boat is the point. Jacket and respect: do not walk onto the beach to chase them.

  • Punta Loma (sea lions; snorkel if the sea allows) Aldo was here

    Why it matters A reserve near Madryn, sea lions year-round. Dive/snorkel with Madrynbuceo if the sea allows (sheet: 1 certified dive ARS 150,000). Wind cancels.

  • Gaiman and Welsh tea Aldo was here

    Why it matters A Welsh town near Trelew, on the Atlantic side (do not mix it up with Trevelin). Tea, black cake, a tea house. A half day from Madryn. Visited Chubut, not the Andean one.

  • Puerto Madryn, the base town Aldo was here

    Why it matters A cold-sea beach, Avenida Roca, sunset on the gulf. The town is not the show; it is where you sleep and eat hake. Summer has people; winter has wind and more table.

  • Lamb, hake, king crab when it is around Aldo was here

    Why it matters The seafood is fresher than fancy. Welsh cake in Gaiman. Patagonian portions, not a tasting menu.

  • Punta Tombo (Magellanic penguins) Tip only — not a map pin

    Why it matters The big mainland colony, south of Trelew, Sep–Mar. Not at Pirámides. A tip: a separate land day (Cuyun Co lists Tombo on the same sheet as Valdés). Do not squeeze it with whales “because it is close” — it is not.

  • Trevelin (the other Chubut) Tip only — not a map pin

    Why it matters Andean west, ~25 km from Esquel, mountain Welsh tea, not whales. I marked Puerto Madryn. Detail in tips. From Bariloche ~4–5 h on RN40.

  • Puerto Madryn Ecocentre Aldo was here

    Why it matters A sea museum on the cliff, south of town. Whales, penguins, the gulf explained indoors when the wind cancels outdoors. Confirm hours.

  • MEF Trelew (paleontology museum) Tip only — not a map pin

    Why it matters Patagonian dinosaurs in Trelew, a short hop from Madryn. A tip if there is a gap between Valdés and the flight. Confirm at mef.org.ar; I am not quoting a ticket I have not seen.

  • Punta Delgada / Caleta Valdés Aldo was here

    Why it matters The far end of the peninsula: elephant seals, a lighthouse, further than Pirámides. A car or a long land day. Do not squeeze it with the morning boat.

Hydro Sport — whale watching

Boat from Puerto Pirámides (not from Madryn). Season Jun–Dec; the port closes in wind. Other boats at the pier (Southern Spirit, Tito Bottazzi and more) and 2026 press talks about unified fares: compare, and do not chase the animals. Madryn–Pirámides transfer extra.

Duration 1 h 30 on the water

Includes boarding at Pirámides; transfer and Valdés ticket extra

Madrynbuceo

Aldo named this — diving / snorkel, public info

A Madryn dive centre (40+ years on their own copy). Certified dives and sea-lion snorkel at Punta Loma, year-round if the sea allows. The public sheet that does quote a fare is 1 certified dive ARS 150,000. Snorkel is unpriced there: madryn.org (Aug 2026) estimates ~ARS 230,000; third-party listings ~USD 135–156. Wind cancels. Website only: no email or phone here.

Cuyun Co — Península Valdés / Punta Tombo

Local Madryn agency · public sheet

An Avenida Roca incoming desk, listed by Madryn’s tourism office. Land day to Valdés or Punta Tombo. Lunch and the protected-area ticket extra; the Pirámides boat is optional (and paid separately, at the boats’ unified fare).

Season Valdés year-round; whales Jun–Dec; Tombo Sep–Mar

Duration full day (~7–18, 400+ km on Valdés)

Includes hotel transfer and guide; protected-area ticket, food, and boat extra

ARS 88,000 / ~USD 57 · Valdés or Tombo on the sheet; foreign protected-area ticket ~ARS 50,000 extra (cuyunco.tur.ar tours, Nov 2026)

Eat

Lamb, hake, king crab when it is around. The seafood is fresher than fancy: order gulf fish, not brochure sushi. Patagonian portions.

Welsh cake and tea in Gaiman (a town near Trelew). Do not mix it up with Trevelin, which is Andes and another trip.

Local hack

A car (the sane option) or the Mar y Valle bus to Pirámides. Do not squeeze the whole peninsula into a morning without a car: the animals are far apart.
