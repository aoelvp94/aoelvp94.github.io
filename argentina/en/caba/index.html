Argentina guide / CABA

CABA

The capital is huge and contradictory: cafés, protests, tango, malbec, and an energy that exhausts and charms. It is not “Argentina”: it is a layer.

Visited by Aldo City of Buenos Aires

From BA You are here AEP 20–40 min · EZE 45–75 min

Best time

Southern Hemisphere December is summer, July is winter.

Ideal Sep–Nov and Mar–May. Avoid Dec–Feb if you don’t like humid heat and storms; Jun–Aug is cool and grey, not extreme.

Getting there

EZE (international) or AEP (domestic and some regional). AEP 20–40 min to the centre; EZE 45–75 min. Tienda León / Uber. Inside the city: SUBE, subway, buses, DiDi/Cabify/Uber.

What to do

  • Palermo (parks, bars), San Telmo (Sunday fair), Recoleta (cemetery, museums).
  • Puerto Madero for the photo, La Boca briefly with your things zipped.
  • Free walking tours at Plaza de Mayo/San Telmo (tip). Tango in a real milonga if you are up for it, not only a dinner show.

Tours and tickets (public prices)

There is no single operator here I can name with a public rate sheet. Park or outing: confirm on the ground and read this season’s reviews.

Eat

Parrilla (Don Julio in Palermo, book ahead), bodegón (El Preferido de Palermo), thick-crust pizza at Güerrín, historic cafés Tortoni and Los 36 Billares, choripán at fairs (La Choripanería in Mercado de San Telmo). Dulce de leche ice cream at any serious heladería.

Local hack

Tigre ~50 min on the Mitre train from Retiro (~ARS 790 with a registered SUBE, Oct 2026). La Plata ~1 h 16 from Constitución. San Antonio de Areco ~2 h. Tandil is not a day trip. Montevideo: there is an overnight EGA bus, not only Buquebus — see the hub hacks.
