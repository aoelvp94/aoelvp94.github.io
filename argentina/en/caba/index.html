Argentina guide / CABA

CABA

The capital is huge and contradictory: cafés, protests, tango, malbec, and an energy that exhausts and charms. It is not “Argentina”: it is a layer.

Visited by Aldo City of Buenos Aires

From BA You are here AEP 20–40 min · EZE 45–75 min

Best time

Southern Hemisphere December is summer, July is winter.

Ideal Sep–Nov and Mar–May. Avoid Dec–Feb if you don’t like humid heat and storms; Jun–Aug is cool and grey, not extreme.

Getting there

EZE (international) or AEP (domestic and some regional). AEP 20–40 min to the centre; EZE 45–75 min. Tienda León / Uber. Inside the city: SUBE, subway, buses, DiDi/Cabify/Uber.

What to do

  • Palermo (parks, bars), San Telmo (Sunday fair), Recoleta (cemetery: Sarmiento is there; card on the hub).
  • Plaza de Mayo and the Metropolitan Cathedral: San Martín’s tomb is inside (right aisle). The walking tour usually passes; no separate ticket. Short historical cards on the hub.
  • Puerto Madero for the photo, La Boca briefly with your things zipped.
  • Free walking tours at Plaza de Mayo/San Telmo (tip). Tango in a real milonga if you are up for it, not only a dinner show.

Tours and tickets (public prices)

USD at ~ARS 1,550 (BNA, Nov 2026). Pesos move: use the number to know whether you are talking about a coffee or a salary. Public operator or APN fares; if there is no sheet, GetYourGuide / Viator / local forums — not an invented price.

Free walking tour (tip-based)

Several companies leave from Plaza de Mayo or San Telmo, 2–3 h, English and Spanish. No fare: you tip at the end. I am not locking one as “the” walk: read recent reviews on GuruWalk, GetYourGuide, or Viator.

Duration 2–3 h

Includes a walking guide through the centre; a tip at the end, not a ticket

typical tip ~USD 10–20 (some local notes say ARS 2,000–5,000: that is a tip, not a fare) · no single operator sheet (tip-based model, Nov 2026)

Milonga (class + floor)

Real tango: you dance, you don’t watch from a dinner table. Covers and floor codes change by hall. Glorieta de Belgrano is outdoors and unticketed. A dinner show (Viejo Almacén, Señor Tango and the rest) is visitor theatre: different money, different plan.

Duration class 1–2 h; milonga runs late

Includes door cover; drink and shoes sometimes extra

ARS 3,000–8,000 / ~USD 2–5 · cover; beginner class often ARS 4,000–10,000 (door range 2026, not one hall)

Tango dinner show (if you insist)

I am not picking “the” show: cast, menu, and price move. Dinner + show packs ~USD 80–150 depending on the room. Compare GetYourGuide/Viator that week; if you want tango not theatre, go to a milonga.

Duration ~3 h (dinner + show)

Includes usually dinner and the show; extra drinks vary

about USD 80–150 · confirm on GetYourGuide/Viator, not here (market range, Nov 2026)

Operators and local outfits

Recommendations and public info — not paid ads. Fares from official sheets or press; no private booking emails, phones, or invoices. Confirm the card the day you pay.

Free walking tours (GuruWalk and peers)

Recommendation · tip-based, no single operator

Plaza de Mayo / San Telmo, 2–3 h, Spanish and English. I am not locking one brand: read this week’s reviews. You tip at the end (~USD 10–20). BA Free Tour and other GuruWalk/GetYourGuide cards cover the same ground. Not a coach city tour.

Season year-round · better Sep–Nov and Mar–May

Duration 2–3 h

Includes walking guide; a tip at the end, not a ticket

typical tip ~USD 10–20 · no single operator sheet (tip-based model, Nov 2026)

Sturla — Tigre and the Delta

Recommendation · classic Delta boat

The day trip that does work from CABA: Mitre train to Tigre (~50 min, ~ARS 790 with SUBE) and a Delta boat. Sturla lists 5 rivers ARS 27,500 and 6 rivers ARS 36,000; Martín García from Tigre ARS 132,000. A paid BA→Tigre city tour is surplus if the train leaves every few minutes. Tandil is not this plan.

Season year-round · the river does not close

Duration 1–2 h on the water; Martín García is a full day

Includes boat from Tigre or Madero; SUBE train extra

ARS 27,500–132,000 / ~USD 18–85 · 5 rivers 27,500 · 6 rivers 36,000 · Martín García 132,000 (sturlaviajes.tur.ar, Nov 2026)

Eat

Parrilla (Don Julio in Palermo, book ahead), bodegón (El Preferido de Palermo), thick-crust pizza at Güerrín, historic cafés Tortoni and Los 36 Billares, choripán at fairs (La Choripanería in Mercado de San Telmo). Dulce de leche ice cream at any serious heladería.

Local hack

Tigre ~50 min on the Mitre train from Retiro (~ARS 790 with a registered SUBE, Oct 2026). La Plata ~1 h 16 from Constitución. San Antonio de Areco ~2 h. Tandil is not a day trip. Montevideo: there is an overnight EGA bus, not only Buquebus — see the hub hacks.
