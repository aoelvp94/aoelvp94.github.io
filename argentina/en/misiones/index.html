Argentina guide / Misiones

Misiones

Jungle, humidity, and the white noise of the falls. Puerto Iguazú is the base; the park is the show. Bug spray, water, shoes that can get wet.

Visited by Aldo Litoral · Iguazú and jungle

From BA Bus ~16–20 h · overnight Flight ~1 h 45–2 h

Best time

Southern Hemisphere December is summer, July is winter.

Year-round (the jungle is always humid). Ideal Apr–Jun and Aug–Oct: less of an oven, solid water volume. Don’t skip it “because it’s winter”: the park is open. Dec–Feb is hottest, with more storms.

Getting there

A flight to IGR (~1 h 45–2 h from Buenos Aires) usually beats the bus (~16–20 h, overnight). Posadas is closer by bus (~12–15 h). Transfers run from the airport to town and the park.

What to do

  • Iguazú National Park: upper and lower circuits plus Garganta del Diablo (the walkways you do on your own). Go early.
  • Puerto Iguazú and the Triple Frontier marker at sunset: the base town, not a replacement for the park.
  • Cross to the Brazilian park if you have time and papers: it is the panoramic postcard, not a replacement for the Argentine side. Do not squeeze both into one day.
  • San Ignacio Miní sits near Posadas, not the falls — a tip, not a pin. If you are already in Posadas, Encarnación (Paraguay) is across the bridge.

Must-try

A local cheat sheet, not a paid list. Aldo was here is the visited pin for this page. Tip only — not a map pin is a reader tip: existing tip blocks below stay tips. Public fares only when a sheet is known; otherwise no invented number.

  • Iguazú National Park — upper and lower circuits Aldo was here

    Why it matters The Argentine boardwalks: jungle, white noise, spray. Go early. The ecological train comes with the ticket. Garganta sometimes closes with high water: check the notice that morning.

    ARS 60,000 / ~USD 39 · general fee; Argentine residents ARS 25,000 · ecological train included, boat extra (APN from 1 Jun 2026)

  • Garganta del Diablo Aldo was here

    Why it matters The balcony over the main drop. The reason to arrive early and not bet everything on the afternoon. If it is closed, the other circuits still pay for the day.

  • Puerto Iguazú and the Triple Frontier marker Aldo was here

    Why it matters The base town: the avenida, sunset at the marker with Paraguay and Brazil in front. It does not replace the park; it closes the day if you still have light.

  • Iguazú Jungle — Gran Aventura (boat) Aldo was here

    Why it matters The Argentine-side boat: jungle in an open vehicle + rapids to the base of the falls. You get soaked. Age 12+, original ID/passport. Park extra. It is not the APN ticket.

    ARS 100,000 / ~USD 65 · excursion only; park extra (iguazujungle.com, Nov 2026)

  • River fish, mandioca, chipá Aldo was here

    Why it matters Litoral plus Brazilian/Paraguayan influence. Surubí or dorado when available. Food inside the park is expensive: bring something. Chipá in town, not on the boardwalk.

  • Brazilian park (Cataratas do Iguaçu) Tip only — not a map pin

    Why it matters The panoramic postcard, not a replacement for the Argentine side. Passport; Rio Uruguay / Crucero del Norte bus to “Cataratas Brasil”. Ask the driver to stop at immigration. Do not squeeze both parks into one day. Ciudad del Este is a different crossing.

  • San Ignacio Miní Tip only — not a map pin

    Why it matters UNESCO Jesuit mission, a different Misiones from the falls. Near Posadas, not Puerto Iguazú. I did not mark it as a pin: a tip if the flight or bus drops you in the south of the province.

  • Güirá Oga Tip only — not a map pin

    Why it matters A jungle-wildlife rescue centre between town and the park. A tip if you have a half day left and you already did the park. Confirm the visit on their site; it is not a photo zoo.

  • Encarnación (Paraguay), if you are already in Posadas Tip only — not a map pin

    Why it matters Across the bridge: chipá and another currency. Not a substitute for the falls. A border tip, not a pin on this page.

  • La Aripuca Tip only — not a map pin

    Why it matters A giant Guaraní trap, jungle timber, between town and the park. Touristy and didactic. A tip if you have a while left and Güirá Oga is done or shut.

  • Wanda mines Tip only — not a map pin

    Why it matters Crystals and geodes a short hop south of Iguazú. A geology tip, not a park replacement. Confirm the visit; I am not quoting a ticket I have not seen on a sheet.

  • Moconá Falls Tip only — not a map pin

    Why it matters Longitudinal falls on the Uruguay, another Misiones: 4×4, a boat, far from Puerto Iguazú. I did not mark it. A tip if the plan is Posadas / El Soberbio, not a day trip from the falls.

Tours and tickets (public prices)

USD at ~ARS 1,550 (BNA, Nov 2026). Pesos move: use the number to know whether you are talking about a coffee or a salary. Public operator or APN fares; if there is no sheet, GetYourGuide / Viator / local forums — not an invented price.

Iguazú National Park ticket (APN)

Includes the ecological train and the circuits open that day. Buy official; the park ticket is not the boat. Garganta sometimes closes with high water: check the park notice that morning.

Duration your park day (go early)

Includes ecological train and open boardwalks; boat extra

ARS 60,000 / ~USD 39 · general fee; Argentine residents ARS 25,000 (APN from 1 Jun 2026)

Iguazú Jungle — Gran Aventura

Yacaratiá trail in an open vehicle + a boat up the rapids to the base of the falls. You will get soaked. Minimum age 12, original ID/passport, boarding restrictions (pregnancy, mobility, etc.). Park and transfers extra. Book in the morning: slots go.

Duration ~2 h (operator; some write-ups say ~2 h 15)

Includes bilingual guide, vehicle, boat, dry bag; no rain poncho or park ticket

ARS 100,000 / ~USD 65 · excursion only; park extra (iguazujungle.com / tickets.iguazujungle.com, Nov 2026)

Eat

Litoral plus Brazilian/Paraguayan influence: river fish (surubí, dorado when available), mandioca, chipá. Order that in town, not a steak you could eat anywhere.

Food inside the park is expensive and the queues are long: bring water and something. Chipá in Puerto Iguazú beats the boardwalk sandwich.

Local hack

Rio Uruguay / Crucero del Norte bus to “Cataratas Brasil”, about hourly from ~7:30. Passport. Ask the driver to stop at Brazilian immigration. Do not squeeze both parks into one day. Ciudad del Este is a different crossing.
