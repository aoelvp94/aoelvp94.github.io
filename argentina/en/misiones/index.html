Argentina guide / Misiones

Misiones

Jungle, humidity, and the white noise of the falls. Puerto Iguazú is the base; the park is the show. Bug spray, water, shoes that can get wet.

Visited by Aldo Litoral · Iguazú and jungle

From BA Bus ~16–20 h · overnight Flight ~1 h 45–2 h

Best time

Southern Hemisphere December is summer, July is winter.

Year-round (the jungle is always humid). Ideal Apr–Jun and Aug–Oct: less of an oven, solid water volume. Don’t skip it “because it’s winter”: the park is open. Dec–Feb is hottest, with more storms.

Getting there

A flight to IGR (~1 h 45–2 h from Buenos Aires) usually beats the bus (~16–20 h, overnight). Posadas is closer by bus (~12–15 h). Transfers run from the airport to town and the park.

What to do

  • Iguazú National Park: upper and lower circuits plus Garganta del Diablo (the walkways you do on your own).
  • Cross to the Brazilian park if you have time and papers: it is the panoramic postcard, not a replacement for the Argentine side.
  • If you are already in Posadas, Encarnación (Paraguay) is across the bridge: chipá and another currency, not a substitute for the falls.

Tours and tickets (public prices)

USD at ~ARS 1,550 (BNA, Nov 2026). Pesos move: use the number to know whether you are talking about a coffee or a salary. Public operator or APN fares; if there is no sheet, GetYourGuide / Viator / local forums — not an invented price.

Iguazú National Park ticket (APN)

Includes the ecological train and the circuits open that day. Buy official; the park ticket is not the boat. Garganta sometimes closes with high water: check the park notice that morning.

Duration your park day (go early)

Includes ecological train and open boardwalks; boat extra

ARS 60,000 / ~USD 39 · general fee; Argentine residents ARS 25,000 (APN from 1 Jun 2026)

Iguazú Jungle — Gran Aventura

Yacaratiá trail in an open vehicle + a boat up the rapids to the base of the falls. You will get soaked. Minimum age 12, original ID/passport, boarding restrictions (pregnancy, mobility, etc.). Park and transfers extra. Book in the morning: slots go.

Duration ~2 h (operator; some write-ups say ~2 h 15)

Includes bilingual guide, vehicle, boat, dry bag; no rain poncho or park ticket

ARS 100,000 / ~USD 65 · excursion only; park extra (iguazujungle.com / tickets.iguazujungle.com, Nov 2026)

Eat

Litoral plus Brazilian/Paraguayan influence: river fish (surubí, dorado when available), mandioca, chipá. Food inside the park is expensive: bring something.

Local hack

Rio Uruguay / Crucero del Norte bus to “Cataratas Brasil”, about hourly from ~7:30. Passport. Ask the driver to stop at Brazilian immigration. Do not squeeze both parks into one day. Ciudad del Este is a different crossing.
