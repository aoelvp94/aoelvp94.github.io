Argentina guide / Tierra del Fuego

Tierra del Fuego

A mountain stuck in the sea, wind that rearranges your hair, and a town that lives off tourism and being the southernmost point most people will ever stand on. For glacier trekking I used Taun, a local Ushuaia operator: Vinciguerra is the long day; Esmeralda is the easier classic.

Visited by Aldo The end of the world · Ushuaia

From BA Flight ~3 h 30 Overland: 2 days

Best time

Southern Hemisphere December is summer, July is winter.

Trekking and the Beagle: Dec–Mar (penguins Oct–Mar). Ski (Cerro Castor): Jun–Aug. Jun–Jul if the idea is a trail: wind and ~7 h of light — it is possible (Vinciguerra runs year-round) but the day is short. Summer ~6–14 °C; a jacket, twelve months. August is Fuegian winter: snow on the trail, crampons or snowshoes. Town and Taun in Ushuaia.

Getting there

Fly to USH (~3 h 30). Overland is two days and a border: almost nobody does it as a visit. The airport (Malvinas Argentinas) is ~4 km / 10–15 min from the centre. No city bus enters the airport peninsula (Oct 2026 note): taxi or remis at arrivals, or a booked transfer. Public shared-transfer listings sit around ARS 20,000 per person (min. 2, Oct 2026–Apr 2027) — confirm that week; it is not a meter.

What to do

  • Tierra del Fuego National Park (coastal trails; the End of the World Train is a different ticket).
  • Trekking with Taun: Vinciguerra (glacier, long day) or Laguna Esmeralda (easier). Public cards on taunushuaia.com.ar.
  • Beagle Channel (lighthouse, sea lions; penguins Oct–Mar on Isla Martillo, no landing).
  • Cerro Martial. In winter, ski at Castor; in summer, long treks.

Ushuaia: town, glacier, how to stack the days

Ushuaia is small and expensive. The centre is walkable: Maipú to the port, the hills to the north. Wind outranks the clock. One glacier day + one Beagle + the park is already a trip; do not stack Vinciguerra and Martillo on the same day.

Taun, the operator I used for glacier

A local Ushuaia operator — mountain guides born here, not a hotel desk. Public cards on taunushuaia.com.ar. I did the Vinciguerra Glacier trek one August (Fuegian winter): snow, short light, and wind can cancel. I recommend them for glacier trekking. Laguna Esmeralda is the easier sibling if the group is not up for 14 km and 650 m. Ojo del Albino and packrafting live on the same site; I do not quote them if I have not seen that day’s card. No phone, email, address, or booking code here.

What it costs, in ranges

  • Vinciguerra — Taun card Nov 2026: ARS 190,000 / ~USD 123. Third-party agencies sometimes ask ~ARS 235,000 (listings Oct 2026–Apr 2027). In 2025 this kind of day sat on the order of a few hundred thousand ARS — a range, not an invoice. Confirm on the card the day you pay.
  • Laguna Esmeralda — ARS 140,000 / ~USD 90 on the Nov 2026 card.
  • National park — APN ARS 40,000 / ~USD 26 general (from 1 Jun 2026). The train is a different ticket.
  • Beagle penguins — Canoero ARS 213,000 / ~USD 137 with tax, Oct–Mar (valid through 31 Dec 2026). The short sea-lion/lighthouse run covers more months.

Getting around

  • Airport — ~4 km, 10–15 min. No city bus onto the peninsula (Oct 2026). Taxi/remis at arrivals; shared-transfer listings ~ARS 20,000 per person (min. 2). I am not inventing a meter total.
  • Town — On foot. A taxi if it rains or if the hotel sits up the hill.
  • Park / Martial / Castor — Tour transfer, remis, or a car. The park to the west is not a block away.

A circuit that closes

  • Day 1 — USH, town, Martial or nothing. Do not stack Vinciguerra on the flight day.
  • Day 2 — Vinciguerra (long) or Esmeralda (shorter). One.
  • Day 3 — Beagle, or the national park, or ski if it is winter. The crossing to Punta Arenas eats a whole day: do not fold it into a 48 h visit (see the hack).

Eat

King crab, black hake, Fuegian lamb. It is expensive compared with the north: you feel the freight. Order sea at the harbour. I am not locking a restaurant that changes hands.

Maipú is the table street. A glacier day wants an early dinner: winter wind and 7 h of light do not forgive. Beer and chocolate exist; they are not the plate.

Local hack

Ushuaia ↔ Punta Arenas: Bus-Sur / Tecni Austral, two borders and a ~30 min Magellan Strait ferry (Punta Delgada). ~USD 40–70 / CLP 58–68k (2026 catalogue). There is no direct ferry. Skip it if you only have two days in Ushuaia.
