Argentina guide / Tierra del Fuego

Tierra del Fuego

A mountain stuck in the sea, wind that rearranges your hair, and a town that lives off tourism and being the southernmost point most people will ever stand on.

Visited by Aldo The end of the world · Ushuaia

From BA Flight ~3 h 30 Overland: 2 days

Best time

Southern Hemisphere December is summer, July is winter.

Trekking and the Beagle: Dec–Mar. Ski: Jun–Aug. Avoid Jun–Jul if the idea is a trail: wind and ~7 h of light. A jacket, every month.

Getting there

Fly to USH (~3 h 30). Overland is two days and a border: almost nobody does it as a visit. The airport is next to town.

What to do

  • Tierra del Fuego National Park.
  • Beagle Channel (lighthouses, sea lions, cormorants).
  • Laguna Esmeralda, Cerro Martial. In winter, skiing; in summer, long treks.

Tours and tickets (public prices)

USD at ~ARS 1,550 (BNA, Nov 2026). Pesos move: use the number to know whether you are talking about a coffee or a salary.

Tierra del Fuego National Park (APN)

The park west of town. Official gate fee.

ARS 40,000 / ~USD 26 · general fee; Argentine residents ARS 18,000 (APN from 1 Jun 2026)

Taun (local mountain guides)

Trekking, Vinciguerra glacier, packrafting, Laguna Esmeralda. For the Beagle, several boats at the port: compare length and whether penguin island is included (seasonal). Check the forecast: wind cancels. Confirm their fare with them.

runs depend on weather (confirm at taunushuaia.com.ar)

Eat

King crab, black hake, Fuegian lamb. It is expensive compared with the north: you feel the freight.

Local hack

Ushuaia ↔ Punta Arenas: Bus-Sur / Tecni Austral, two borders and a ~30 min Magellan Strait ferry (Punta Delgada). ~USD 40–70 / CLP 58–68k (2026 catalogue). There is no direct ferry. Skip it if you only have two days in Ushuaia.
