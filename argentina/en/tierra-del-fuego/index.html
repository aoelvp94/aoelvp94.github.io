Argentina guide / Tierra del Fuego

Tierra del Fuego

A mountain stuck in the sea, wind that rearranges your hair, and a town that lives off tourism and being the southernmost point most people will ever stand on.

Visited by Aldo The end of the world · Ushuaia

From BA Flight ~3 h 30 Overland: 2 days

Best time

Southern Hemisphere December is summer, July is winter.

Trekking and the Beagle: Dec–Mar. Ski: Jun–Aug. Avoid Jun–Jul if the idea is a trail: wind and ~7 h of light. A jacket, every month.

Getting there

Fly to USH (~3 h 30). Overland is two days and a border: almost nobody does it as a visit. The airport is next to town.

What to do

  • Tierra del Fuego National Park.
  • Beagle Channel (lighthouses, sea lions, cormorants).
  • Laguna Esmeralda, Cerro Martial. In winter, skiing; in summer, long treks.

Tours and tickets (public prices)

USD at ~ARS 1,550 (BNA, Nov 2026). Pesos move: use the number to know whether you are talking about a coffee or a salary. Public operator or APN fares; if there is no sheet, GetYourGuide / Viator / local forums — not an invented price.

Tierra del Fuego National Park (APN)

The park west of town. Official gate fee. Coastal trails; the End of the World Train is a different ticket, not the park.

Duration your park day

Includes open trails; train and Beagle extra

ARS 40,000 / ~USD 26 · general fee; Argentine residents ARS 18,000 (APN from 1 Jun 2026)

Taun — Laguna Esmeralda

Local mountain guides (EPGAMT/AAGM). The easy trekking classic: 9 km, 300 m. Vinciguerra is a different, harder card. Packrafting: confirm on the site. Wind cancels.

Duration 5 h · 9 km · 300 m · year-round

Includes hotel transfers, certified guide, packed lunch, insurance, poles and crampons/snowshoes; clothes and boots extra

ARS 140,000 / ~USD 90 · listed in ARS on the card; the site also shows USD (taunushuaia.com.ar, Nov 2026)

Taun — Vinciguerra Glacier trek

The same local operator as Laguna Esmeralda. A long day: Valle de Andorra, peat bog, native forest, Laguna de los Témpanos and the glacier. Moderate grade: a steady pace, not a sprint. The ice is receding and the caves change — confirm what is open that day. Wind cancels.

Duration 8 h · 14 km · 650 m · moderate · year-round

Includes transfers, technical kit (poles, crampons/snowshoes, helmet), food, certified guide, insurance; warm clothes, boots and bottle extra

ARS 190,000 / ~USD 123 · listed on the operator card; third-party listings sometimes show another number (taunushuaia.com.ar/excursion/glaciar-vinciguerra/, Nov 2026)

Canoero — Beagle penguins (Isla Martillo)

Catamaran from the pier: sea lions, Les Eclaireurs lighthouse, and Isla Martillo. No landing on the island. Oct–Mar. The short Isla de los Lobos run (no penguins) is ARS 123,000 / ~3 h, year-round. Tolkeyen and other boats at the port: compare, do not chase wildlife. Wind cancels.

Duration ~5 h (Oct–Mar)

Includes navigation + ARS 6,000 port tax; transfer and landing extra / not offered

ARS 213,000 / ~USD 137 · per person, tax included; ages 4–11 pay 50% (canoerocatamaranes.com.ar, valid through 31 Dec 2026)

Eat

King crab, black hake, Fuegian lamb. It is expensive compared with the north: you feel the freight.

Local hack

Ushuaia ↔ Punta Arenas: Bus-Sur / Tecni Austral, two borders and a ~30 min Magellan Strait ferry (Punta Delgada). ~USD 40–70 / CLP 58–68k (2026 catalogue). There is no direct ferry. Skip it if you only have two days in Ushuaia.
